What Is EOTM?

Turning Compassion
into Action

The End of the Month (EOTM) Celebration is one of EHI's most consistent traditions. On the last Sunday of every month, our volunteers visit orphanages across Lagos to create genuine moments of joy, hope, and companionship for children who are often forgotten by society.

In 2026, EHI is scaling this to 10 orphanages (January to October), with each visit hosting 50–80 children and including meals, supplies, learning materials, games, and celebration.

"These moments reflect who we are: people helping people with purpose, presence, and love."
