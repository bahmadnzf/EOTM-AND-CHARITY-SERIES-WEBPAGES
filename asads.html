Our Story

Born from a calling to heal

Founded in 2018 by Mr. Dare Asobele under the Executives Helping Initiative, ASADS began with just 9 volunteers and a bold vision to dramatically reduce depression and suicide in Nigeria. Today, it operates as a structured, multi-department organization powered by trained mental health advocates, professional therapists, and passionate listeners committed to the well-being of every Nigerian.

Depression is not a weakness. Suicide is not a solution. With awareness, training, and reach-out programs, ASADS walks alongside those who are struggling until they find light.

Vision 2030
Significantly reducing depression, suicide, and mental health issues in Nigeria by 50% before 2030.
Mission
Reaching as many people as possible through awareness campaigns, training, and workshops, encouraging help-seeking and reducing stigma.

Community

The ASADS Forums

Four active online communities where mental health discussions happen daily. A safe, moderated space for teachings, self-help tips, inspiration, and connection, all content held in strict confidence.

Forums are administered by the Coordinator, Assistant Coordinator, and Head of Human Resources. Daily inspirational quotes, mental health teachings, and self-help resources are shared every day.

Forum Guidelines
  • Keep posts relevant to mental health and community well-being.
  • Avoid data-heavy media (large videos or audio files) that strain members' data.
  • No advertisements: seek coordinator approval before sharing any promotions.
  • Resolve grievances privately; the forum is not for airing disputes.
  • Be kind, respectful, and open to the experiences and opinions of others.
  • Every voice counts: create space for every member to contribute.
