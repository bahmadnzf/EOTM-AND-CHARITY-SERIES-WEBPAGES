MEET OUR FOUNDER

Dare Asobele

Founder & Visionary

Lawyer Project Manager Change Catalyst

Timothy-Asobele Emmanuel Dare popularly known as Dare Asobele, founded Executives Helping Initiative in 2013, while studying law at the University of Lagos. What sparked this journey? A moment of Anger that was transformed into action. After visiting an orphanage and realizing good intentions were not enough. Moved by this, legal icons like Chief Gani Fawehinmi and Justice Chukwudifu Oputa, he brought together five friends and turned compassion into what would grow into a national movement.

Called to the Nigerian Bar (2015) | PMP® Certified

Also founded ASADS (Anti-Suicide and Depression Squad)

The world just needs people willing to take action.
