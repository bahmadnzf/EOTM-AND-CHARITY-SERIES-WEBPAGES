WHAT WE DO

The Next Generation of Change Agents

Founded in 2013, Executives Helping Initiative is a youth-led movement fighting poverty through action. We show up at orphanages, prisons, hospitals, and underserved communities across Lagos — delivering food, medical care, hope, and second chances.

We don't just raise awareness. We implement solutions.

MEET OUR FOUNDER

The Visionary Behind the Movement

Dare Asobele
Founder & Visionary
Lawyer Project Manager Change Catalyst

Timothy-Asobele Emmanuel Dare, popularly known as Dare Asobele, founded Executives Helping Initiative in 2013 while studying law at the University of Lagos. What sparked this journey? A moment of anger transformed into action — after visiting an orphanage and realizing good intentions were not enough. Moved by legal icons like Chief Gani Fawehinmi and Justice Chukwudifu Oputa, he brought together five friends and turned compassion into what would grow into a national movement.

Called to the Nigerian Bar (2015) | PMP® Certified

Also founded ASADS (Anti-Suicide and Depression Squad)

The world just needs people willing to take action.
