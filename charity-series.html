About The Charity Series

Every Year,
We Show Up.

Every year, EHI runs The Charity Series three back-to-back events that take us from our screens, to prison walls, to the streets of an underserved community.

In 2026, we are raising funds online for people battling cancer, sickle cell disease, and mental health challenges. We are walking into Kirikiri and Ikoyi Prisons to feed, encourage, and restore hope to 1,500 inmates. And we are showing up in Aiyetoro Community, Yaba, to give over 300 families food, clothing, medical care, and a reason to celebrate.

Your donation , no matter the size ,makes every one of these moments possible. Founded on 26 December 2013, EHI has grown from visiting orphanages into a global movement with over 1,000 volunteers. Our monthly outreach commitment remains unchanged — because those who need help cannot wait.
